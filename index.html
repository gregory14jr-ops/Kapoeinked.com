The Artist

Leaving My Mark Here

Hser Eh Wei is a native of Chapel Hill, NC — a first-generation immigrant to parents who came from Myanmar. Eh (E-a) has always grown up in love with creating. Starting his apprenticeship at 18, he took the unconventional route most kids of immigrants could only dream of. Now, as an established artist, he hopes to create one-of-a-kind work for all his amazing clients. Truly blessed by friends, family, and community, he hopes to give back and build a pathway for others on a similar journey.

Healed work, studio life, and client stories all live on Instagram. It's the best place to see the range of what's possible before your own personal consultation.
